# Home Energy Management System

## Overview

Bachelor's thesis project completed during the B.Tech in Electrical and Electronics Engineering at VIT Chennai. The project used MATLAB and Simulink to model a home energy management system.

**Focus:** Home energy management and simulation-based engineering analysis.  
**Tools:** MATLAB and Simulink.

**Repository status:** Summary only. Model files, system architecture, simulation instructions and results can be documented when the original project materials are available.
